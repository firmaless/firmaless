<p align="center"><b>Sign a PDF without printing it.</b></p>

FirmaLess replaces "print, sign by hand, scan, send". You photograph your own handwritten signature once, place it on your PDF, and download a document that looks like a signed paper scan.

**Website:** [firmaless.com](https://firmaless.com) · English and Italian · currently in beta

## Your documents stay on your device

- Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF is opened, signed and saved on your own computer.
- Nothing is uploaded: not the document, not the photo of your signature.
- The part of the app that handles your documents is blocked from the network by your browser, and it keeps working with your connection turned off.

FirmaLess is for signing with **your own signature**.

## ⚠️ Everything here is public

**Never attach a real document, a signature, or a screenshot that shows either.** Anything posted here can be seen and copied by anyone, and stays in the history even after an edit.

If one particular PDF fails, describe it instead: where it came from (a bank, a scanner app, a word processor), how many pages it has, whether it is protected by a password. If we need the file itself, we will arrange a private way to receive it.

If you posted something by mistake, tell us in the issue and we will delete it.
